Personal Reflection-Isadora Terziu
What suprised me the most?
During my research on Microsoft and Google.I was mosty suprised by the massive scale at which these companies have integrated AI into their daily workflows.It is impressive that tools like GitHub Copilot are not just being used to write code faster,but also to assist in project documentation and eliminate repetetive skills.
What concerns me about AI in software engineering?
My main concern is the risk of over-dependence on these tools.As I observed from the research,AI can often generate erroneous code or "hallucinations".If junior engineers start accepting AI-generated code without fully understanding how it works, it could lead to major security issues and a degradation of our professional skills
What opportunities do zou see?
On the other hand,AI opens up incredible opportunities.By freeing us from writing simple or repetetive code, we as engineers can focus more on the core application logic,system design,and solving complex problems.This allows us to be much more creative.
How will I personally adapt?
I will personally adapt by using AI tools as assistants rather than replacement.I will focus my energy on throughlty understanding the fundamentals of programming,software architecture,and critical thinking,because these are human skills that AI cannot replace.My goal is to remain an engineer who controls and verifies the code,rather than someone who simplz copies and pastes.
